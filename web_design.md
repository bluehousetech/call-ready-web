# Call Ready
    
    ## Target Audience
    Small service businesses—mechanics, plumbers, electricians, contractors, and other tradespeople who work in the field and frequently miss calls while on job sites.
    
    ## What It Provides
    An intelligent voicemail and call routing system that ensures businesses never miss an opportunity. When you can't answer:

    - Smart routing based on business hours, holidays, and custom conditions
    - Professional voicemail with customizable greetings and prompts
    - Automatic transcription of voicemail messages
    - Instant SMS notifications to on-call employees with message details
    - Customer follow-up via automated SMS with booking links or callback information
    - Call logging and recording storage for business records

    ## Key Differentiator
    Veteran-owned and built specifically for working professionals who need reliable, no-nonsense communication when they're hands-on with a job. Every missed call becomes a captured lead with an action plan.

    ## Tagline: "Always on call. Always ready."
    Target: Mechanics, plumbers, contractors, electricians
    Positioning: Veteran-owned, built for people who work with their hands
    Value prop: Never miss a job while you're on a job

# NextJS Technical Overview
    ## Tech Stack
    - Framework: Next.js 14+ (App Router)
    - Styling: Tailwind CSS
    - Deployment: Vercel (seamless Next.js integration)
    - CMS (optional): Sanity or Contentful (for blog/resources)
    - Analytics: Vercel Analytics or Google Analytics
    - Forms: React Hook Form + server actions
